Paletto

Extract, create, and manage beautiful color palettes on iOS.

Features

  • Extract palettes from photos and your camera
  • Color harmony generation (complementary, triadic, and more)
  • Color blindness simulation for accessible design
  • Contrast matrix for WCAG compliance checking
  • Compare palettes side by side
  • Export palettes in multiple formats
  • Share palettes via QR code
  • Home screen widget for quick access
