Privacy Policy

Paletto for iOS

Effective date: March 17, 2026

Overview

Paletto is designed with privacy at its core. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Data Collection

Paletto does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reporting sent to external servers
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Camera & Photo Library

Paletto may request access to your camera and photo library. These permissions are used solely to extract colors from images you choose. Photos are processed on-device and are never uploaded or shared.

Local Storage

Paletto uses the following on-device storage:

  • UserDefaults — stores your app preferences and settings locally on your device.
  • App Group container — shares palette data between the app and its home screen widget. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Paletto does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect or process user data.

Children's Privacy

Paletto does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
